Endpoint Connect Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

1. Scope and contact

This Privacy Policy describes how Endpoint Connect (the “App”), an independently developed Android AI client, handles information when you use its features. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to Endpoint Connect's developer.

For privacy questions, support-related requests, or requests concerning information we hold directly, contact endpointconnect@gmail.com.

Applicable data protection rights and obligations, including those under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 where applicable, continue to apply.

2. Overview

Endpoint Connect allows you to use AI providers and endpoints you configure. It does not operate its own hosted AI inference proxy or cloud conversation account. Ordinary AI requests are sent from your device to the selected providers, endpoints or connected services, not through an Endpoint Connect inference server.

  • Endpoint Connect does not display advertising or sell personal data.
  • No Endpoint Connect cloud account is required.
  • Conversations, attachments, settings and connection details are primarily stored on your device, subject to your device and backup configuration.
  • Using hosted AI, speech, image, video, search or tool features can transmit relevant content to external services.
  • Some secondary requests can occur automatically, depending on features and permissions you enable.
  • Supported local AI and speech features can operate without transmitting their processing content to a hosted AI inference service.

3. Information the App handles

3.1 Connections and credentials

You may enter or authorize provider URLs, API keys, tokens, login details, cookies, model choices and connection settings. The App stores sensitive connection information locally using applicable Android security facilities and protected storage. We do not routinely receive these credentials on a developer-operated server.

Authentication requests and necessary credentials are sent to the provider or endpoint you configure. External sign-in providers, such as supported account or browser-based sign-in services, process their own authentication information under their own policies.

3.2 Chats, Projects and personal context

The App handles prompts, chat history, model responses, drafts, Project instructions, Library items, attachments and settings to provide its features and preserve local history. When you send a hosted AI request, relevant messages and enabled context are transmitted to the selected provider or endpoint.

Optional personalization and Personal Context may include a chosen name, occupation, approximate location or other information you enter. If enabled for a hosted request, relevant fields may be transmitted as part of that request. Those fields can be changed or removed locally through supported App controls.

Providers may process and retain information under their own terms, privacy policies and settings, including policies concerning model training or service improvement.

3.3 Files, photos, audio and video

If you choose to attach, import, capture, generate, edit, export or share content, the App may access selected files, documents, images, microphone recordings and videos to perform the operation. Depending on the feature, selected content may stay on your device or be transmitted to the configured provider or service.

Exporting a file to a cloud storage app or another destination you select may give that destination access to the exported content. Camera, microphone and other Android permissions can be managed through your device settings.

3.4 Voice Chat, dictation and speech processing

Voice Chat is an optional mode you start. During an active session, the App can recognize completed speech turns, automatically send the resulting transcripts to the selected chat provider, and speak AI responses. Individual turns may be submitted without a separate tap on Send.

Where supported, Android dictation and Voice Chat speech recognition use the device's on-device recognizer; availability depends on installed language resources and device capabilities. When you separately select a supported hosted speech-to-text operation, such as an OpenRouter service, audio submitted for that operation is transmitted to that service.

Supported Voice Chat controls allow you to mute, pause or exit a session. Capture is stopped according to the App's session controls and lifecycle when muted, paused, exited or backgrounded. Microphone access remains subject to Android permissions and its privacy indicators. Voice Chat is not designed to record secretly in the background.

Speech transcripts and assistant responses may be retained in the locally stored chat history. Hosted audio is handled transiently within the App for the requested operation; the provider may have separate retention practices.

For speech output, the App can use Android system text-to-speech, compatible hosted speech synthesis services, or supported offline Kyutai Pocket TTS models. Hosted synthesis sends text to the selected provider. An installed local Kyutai engine synthesizes locally without sending that text to a hosted Kyutai inference service. Android system speech engines may have their own network and privacy behavior.

3.5 Image and video generation

When you request image creation, image editing or video generation, supported providers may receive prompts, reference images or other content necessary to produce the requested output. Generated media may be previewed, downloaded, saved locally or exported to a destination you choose. The provider's own retention and processing rules apply.

3.6 External images displayed in chat

Some AI responses reference external images. Displaying an inline image may automatically contact its host, which can receive the requested image address, your network IP address and applicable HTTP request metadata. The host's privacy practices apply. The App does not intentionally attach your AI provider credentials to these image requests.

3.7 Optional models and Google components

Some on-device features require downloadable models or app components delivered through Google Play. Google may process technical installation, device, delivery and security data for these services under its applicable policies.

Supported App features use Google ML Kit components, including for text recognition, language identification or translation. Depending on the component and its configuration, Google documents possible collection of installation or device identifiers, usage events, performance measurements and error or diagnostic information. These SDK activities are distinct from Endpoint Connect operating its own behavioral advertising system.

3.8 Support and AI-generated content reports

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to submit, which may include an email address, written description, screenshots or diagnostic context. Selected in-app AI-generated content reporting features use Basin, a third-party form-processing service, to receive reports for moderation and support review.

For the Higgsfield video reporting flow in v72, the report contains a user-reviewed written description and the limited submission details necessary to process the report. That flow is designed to exclude video bytes, original prompts, job identifiers, media URLs and provider credentials. The App asks the user to review the report before submission.

The form-processing service may also process network and submission metadata, such as an IP address, approximate IP-derived location, request timestamp, and security or anti-abuse information, according to its own operations and privacy terms. Reports may be delivered to our moderation/support inbox. These operations are separate from hosted AI inference.

We retain support correspondence and received reports only as reasonably necessary to review submissions, respond to users, protect the service, keep appropriate records or meet legal obligations. Copies stored by Basin, email providers or other processors may have different retention periods. We do not promise a fixed deletion period unless one has been confirmed for the applicable service and configuration.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of report or support information held by us, contact endpointconnect@gmail.com. Requests are subject to applicable legal requirements and reasonable verification. Requests concerning information held independently by Basin or an AI provider may also need to be addressed to that service.

Do not include API keys, passwords, private prompts or unnecessary sensitive data in a report or support request.

3.9 Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your complete payment card details. Google may provide purchase or transaction status information needed for support and administration under its own privacy practices.

4. How information is used

  • To authenticate with services you select and send or receive AI requests.
  • To provide chat, Projects, personalization, speech, search, generation, editing and other App functions.
  • To save local history, attachments, connections, settings and backups where supported.
  • To operate connected tools and external services as configured.
  • To support security, functionality, diagnostics and model/component delivery.
  • To respond to support requests, handle content reports and meet applicable legal obligations.

5. External providers, search and tools

5.1 Providers and custom endpoints

Depending on your configuration, the App may connect to commercial AI services, OpenRouter, authentication providers, or user-operated services such as Open WebUI, Ollama, LM Studio, LocalAI, vLLM, llama.cpp, Hermes and other compatible endpoints. Their operators independently determine their data-handling practices.

5.2 MCP and automatic secondary requests

Model Context Protocol (MCP) tools and other integrations can forward tool arguments or relevant content to services other than your primary chat provider. Some operations request approval. If you enable automatic approval or equivalent functionality, model-selected tool calls may transmit information without an individual confirmation for each call. Review connected tool permissions and providers before enabling them.

5.3 Web Search and retrieval

If supported search or retrieval functionality is used, a search query or related request information may be transmitted to the configured external search service. A model or tool may determine a search query automatically as part of a broader request. Searches confined to locally stored App content do not by themselves require a hosted search provider.

5.4 Sharing and third-party handling

Information sent to external providers or services may be processed, stored, transferred or disclosed according to those services' agreements and privacy policies. Some transmissions are initiated directly by your actions, while others occur automatically to provide enabled features. Endpoint Connect does not sell personal data. It does not control independently operated providers.

You are responsible for determining whether you are authorized to submit content, including information about other individuals, to the services you select.

6. Security and network connections

The App uses available Android platform protections and secure local storage techniques for sensitive connection information. The security of locally held information also depends on your device, operating system, backups and installed software.

Transport security in Production v72 / 0.1.41: Connections to servers outside the phone, including servers on a local network or VPN, require encrypted HTTPS or WSS transport with a trusted certificate. Unencrypted HTTP and WebSocket connections to these external endpoints are blocked. Same-device loopback connections remain supported for compatible local services.

If you previously configured an HTTP-only LAN, VPN or self-hosted server, you must enable a trusted TLS configuration on the server or place an appropriate HTTPS/WSS reverse proxy in front of it. Some real-time integrations require secure WebSocket upgrade support as well.

Version note: The HTTPS/WSS requirement described above is specific to v72 and later versions using this enforcement. Earlier distributed versions may have allowed cleartext connections to some user-configured private-network endpoints. Their risks and behavior are not changed retroactively by the v72 update.

No security measure eliminates all risk. You should protect your device, use trusted endpoints, avoid disclosing credentials and review the data practices of connected services.

7. Local storage, backups and retention

Local conversations, transcripts, attachments, generated files, Projects, Library items, drafts and settings may remain on the device until you delete them, clear App storage or uninstall the App, subject to backup and recovery behavior. Some Android or device-vendor backup features may retain copies separately.

Deleting local content does not necessarily delete information previously transmitted to a connected provider, search service, tool, form service or cloud export destination. Consult those operators' deletion controls for their copies.

We generally do not hold server-side copies of ordinary conversations because Endpoint Connect does not operate its own cloud chat account or inference server. Support and report information you submit directly to us is handled as described in Section 3.8.

8. Your choices and permissions

You can choose providers, endpoints, models and supported tools; control optional personalization; manage or delete supported locally stored information; and grant or revoke Android permissions. Hosted functions may require provider accounts, network access or paid usage billed by the selected provider.

Some App features need microphone, camera, files, models or network permissions and may not function if permissions are revoked or required components are unavailable. No Endpoint Connect cloud account is needed.

9. International processing

Independent AI providers, search services, authentication providers, SDK operators, form processors and cloud destinations may process data in different countries. Where a selected service handles information, its terms and privacy policy govern its own processing and international transfers, subject to applicable law.

10. Privacy rights and requests

Your rights may include access, correction, deletion, objection, restriction or withdrawal of consent, depending on applicable law. Much App content is stored locally, and you can manage it through available App and Android controls.

For information held by us through direct support communications or reports, email endpointconnect@gmail.com. We may need to verify requests and may retain records where permitted or required by law. For data held by an independently operated AI provider or endpoint, direct the request to that operator.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may revise this Policy as the App, its integrations and applicable law change. We will update the date at the top of the page and provide additional notice if required.
